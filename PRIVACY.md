# Privacy

Short version: **Menu Bar Countdown collects nothing and sends nothing.**

- It saves only the date you count down to and the display you picked (Days, Minutes or Seconds), in the app's own settings on your Mac. Delete the app and those are left as a tiny settings file you can ignore.
- It makes no network requests. There are no servers, accounts, analytics, ads or trackers.
- It asks for no permissions. It only adds itself to Login Items if you tick **Open at Login** in its menu, and you can untick it there or in System Settings → General → Login Items.
